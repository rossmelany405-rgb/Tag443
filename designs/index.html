Designs

City of Cape Town tender award 2008/2009

The Academy of Guilds (TAG443) was awarded Tender 354S/2007/08: the interior design of 8 customer interface areas for the Planning and Building Development Management Directorate.

Client
City of Cape Town, City Council, Western Cape
Scope
TAG443 worked with a team of associates. Its responsibilities covered detail design and documentation, project implementation, and budget spending and management. Two learners from the TAG443 experiential training programme helped with the design and portfolio preparation.
Project duration
18 months
Completion
June 2009
