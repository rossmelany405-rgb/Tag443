Admission

DHET registration

TAG443: The Academy of Guilds (Pty) Ltd {2008/022664/08}, trading as TAG443 College, has achieved provisional registration as a private FET College in terms of section 31(3) of the Act and regulation 12(4)(b) with the Department of Higher Education and Training.

The unique registration number is 2011/FE08/008 and the duration of this registration is until 31 December 2017.

How to apply

The college handles admission to full time and part time courses using an RPL (recognition of prior learning) assessment. Candidates should contact the college for advice and assistance.

Every course has set entrance criteria with a scoring point system, plus an interview at the college. Full time learners need Grade 12 and a portfolio of drawings (see Academia).
