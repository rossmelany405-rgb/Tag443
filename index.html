Admission

22 February 2010 · Posted by Admin

DHET registration: TAG443: The Academy of Guilds, trading as TAG443 College, has achieved provisional registration as a private FET College in terms of section 31(3) of the Act and regulation 12(4)(b) with the Department of Higher Education and Training.

The unique registration number is 2011/FE08/008 and the duration of this registration is until 31 December 2017.

Admission to programmes is administered by the college on an RPL (recognition of prior learning) assessment method, so candidates should contact the college for advice and assistance. All programmes have set entrance criteria with a scoring point system and an interview held at the college.
