Admission

February 22, 2010 @ 2:51 am posted by Admin

DHET REGISTRATION: TAG443:-The Academy of Guilds

trading as TAG443 College has achieved provisional registration as private FET College in terms of section 31(3) of the Act and regulation 12(4)(b) with the Department of Higher Education and Training

The unique registration number is 2011/FE08/008 and the duration of this registration is until the 31st December 2017.

Admission to programmes is administered by the college on a RPL assessment method (recognition of prior learning) and therefore it is advisable for candidates to contact the college for further advice and assistance.

All programmes has set criteria on entrance with a scoring point system and an interview held at the college.
