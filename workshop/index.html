Workshop

TAG443 runs hands-on workshops alongside its full time programmes.

Studio sessions and workshops are a compulsory part of every course (see Academia). The college also offers one-month part time short courses in CAD and rendering software. These are listed under Programmes.

For upcoming workshop dates, please contact the college.
